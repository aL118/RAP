drag on empty canvas draws a box for the
selected track; drag inside moves it; drag a
corner resizes. Any edit makes this frame a
keyframe. Frames between two keyframes are
interpolated; a track shows nothing before its
first or after its last keyframe.

A reject region deletes instead of adding:
every detector box it covers is dropped and it
contributes none of its own. Use it on a false
positive -- the bonnet, a hedge read as a car --
where drawing over would only swap your box in
for theirs.
